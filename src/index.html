The Wall

An experiment for Card Chaser: no tabs, no screens. Your whole collection is one surface, and zooming is the navigation.

  • Tap a set (or spread two fingers on it) to open it. Its cards grow into a binder. Scroll down it; flick sideways for the next set; pinch out or tap back to close it.
  • Tap a card to bring it up close. Flick it sideways to move along the set.
  • Lenses at the bottom recolor the wall: what you need, live deals, what's worth the most, and Time: scrub or play through when you got each card.
  • Search flies you to the cards that match.
  • Press and hold any card to mark it, with Undo. Tap the count to see everything again.
  • Rearrange (top left) lays the same cards out by set, by Pokémon or by value.

1,327 real cards from 10 sets. Ownership, prices and deals are made up for the demo, and your taps are kept on this device only.
