          The Wall

          An experiment for Card Chaser: no tab bar. Your whole collection is one surface, the app's five rooms sit on a map one level above it, and zooming is the navigation.

          • Rooms: pinch the wall closed (or tap the rooms button at the top left) and it shrinks into the map, five rooms on one screen, each showing what's happening in it: Feed (every listing found for your chases, newest first), Chase (this wall), Trade (your trade binder and the trade checker), Trophies (every trophy you've earned) and Source (where it looks). Tap a room or spread two fingers on it to go in; pinch any room closed to come back. Flick sideways in a room for the next one along.
          • Starting out: import your collection from TCGplayer or Collectr, or pick the sets you collect and mark by hand. Choose your sets again any time in Settings.
          • Tap a set (or spread two fingers on it) to open it. Its cards grow into a binder. Scroll down it; flick sideways for the next set; pinch out or tap back to close it.
          • Tap a card to bring it up close. Flick it sideways to move along the set.
          • Collection and Chase at the bottom are lenses: they recolor the wall rather than taking you somewhere else. Collection is the wall as it is, what you have in color and what you're missing as empty pockets.
          • The wall is live. When a copy of a card you chase comes up for less, its tile flashes where it sits and the listing lands in the Feed, which the rooms button counts. Nothing nags.
          • Chase is your chase list: one tile for each card you're after, dealt out of the wall, its best deal leading or the most you'd pay. (Every listing, several per card where there are, is in the Feed.) Tap one and it pops up with every copy online: swipe through them, or switch to the packs and boxes it comes in. Got it asks what you paid. Chase it on any card adds it; On a card you own, + counts a copy: every copy past your first is a spare, up for trade.
          • The Complete Dex is a chase with one slot for every Pokémon, #1 to #1,025, in Dex order. Add it from New chase, or in Choose your sets. A slot fills when you have any card of that Pokémon; the ones your sets don't print read "Not in your sets". Its settings count only holo or full art prints, or one type.
          • Trade (a room) holds the trade binder, every card you have a spare of, most wanted first: swipe its pages, tap a card to trade it, or use Show mode to hand it across a table and let the other person pick. The trade checker says whether a trade is fair, cards or cash, by condition. Tap a collector and the screen becomes a table between you: their spares above, yours below. Drag cards onto the table and shake hands.
          • Trophies: every set and chase earns them as it fills, and now and then one comes up Critical or Shiny. Inside a set the next one hangs on the bar; mark the card that tips it and it mints right there. A finished set becomes a plaque. A trophy sits on the shelf at the top of the wall for its first day, then moves to the Trophies room: tap a trophy to see the cards behind it.
          • Filters (top, by the search): Show all, only what's missing, or only what you have; Color by type or value (the more it's worth, the hotter); Group by set, price, rarity or type, every card flying to its new place; the order in a binder (number, price, name or rarity); and Time, to scrub or play through when you got each card. In Chase, the chase list sorts by best deal, dearest or cheapest. While a filter is on, it's named beside the lenses, with ✕ to clear it.
          • Search flies you to the cards that match.
          • Press and hold any card to mark it, then keep the finger down and sweep along the row. Mark (top, inside a set) does the same by tapping: a handful at once, Done sums it up. Tap the count to see everything again.

          1,327 real cards from 10 sets. Ownership, prices and deals are made up for the demo, and your taps are kept on this device only.
