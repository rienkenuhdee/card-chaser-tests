The Wall

An experiment for Card Chaser: no tabs, no screens. Your whole collection is one surface, and zooming is the navigation.

  • Tap a set (or spread two fingers on it) to open it. Its cards grow into a binder. Scroll down it; flick sideways for the next set; pinch out or tap back to close it.
  • Tap a card to bring it up close. Flick it sideways to move along the set.
  • Have, Need, Chase, Trade at the bottom are lenses: they recolor the wall rather than taking you somewhere else.
  • Chase is your chase list: the cards you're after deal out of the wall as a feed of tiles, live deals first. Tap one and it pops up with every copy online: swipe through them, or switch to the packs and boxes it comes in. Got it asks what you paid. Chase it on any card adds it; Spare on a card you own puts it up for trade.
  • Trade shows your spares and who wants them. Tap a collector and the screen becomes a table between you: their spares above, yours below. Drag cards onto the table and shake hands.
  • Filters (top, by the search) add Value, the more it's worth the hotter, and Time: scrub or play through when you got each card.
  • Search flies you to the cards that match.
  • Press and hold any card to mark it, then keep the finger down and sweep along the row. Mark (top, inside a set) does the same by tapping: a handful at once, Done sums it up. Tap the count to see everything again.
  • The chase (top left) lays the same cards out by set, by Pokémon, by artist or by value.

1,327 real cards from 10 sets. Ownership, prices and deals are made up for the demo, and your taps are kept on this device only.
