# r16-radical-trophy-timeline

**Round:** 16. Trophies: where do they live after their day on the shelf, and what can a trophy do?
**Concept:** The trophies are the collection's history, one level above the wall. Pinch the mosaic itself in and the whole wall shrinks to a thumbnail at "today" while the trophies spread out around it on a line of dates (oldest furthest left, the shelf's fresh ones hanging right beside the wall), with the collection's growth curve behind the line and the moments that aren't trophies as ticks; drag sideways through the years, tap a plaque to open its album from where it hangs, spread to grow the thumbnail back into the mosaic under your fingers.

## What changed
- Added `82-timeline.js` (sorts after the welcome, so its redefinitions win). Nothing in `src/` is touched. It redefines, by hoisting:
  - `pinchMove`, `releasePinch`: on the mosaic a two-finger pinch that closes (r < 0.96, with a trophy on the wall and no transition) commits to the timeline: q = (1 − r) / 0.5 held under the fingers as a `state.trans` of kind `tl`; release snaps by speed first (the same 0.0011 q/ms as a set), position second (past 0.4 opens). A spread still opens the panel under the fingers, exactly as before; the gesture commits to one direction. With no trophy, `tlReady()` is false and the pinch does nothing, as today.
  - `frame`: a copy with two additions: the `tl` transition (the wall drawn through a scale from the screen to its thumbnail, the plaques gliding from their place on the wall to their place on the line, the line and labels fading in over the second half) and the `timeline` view. An album opened from a plaque on the line uses the base `open` transition with the timeline as its fading backdrop (`T.tl`).
  - `drawMosaic`, `layoutAll`, `drawCaseLabel`, `tap`, `openTrans`, `settled`, `setChrome`: the thumbnail is the mosaic drawn once into an offscreen image (in screen-high slices on the main canvas, copied off, with half and quarter size copies; plaques and the case label left out so they can travel) and kept until the layout, lens, filter, size or theme changes. The case label now reads "Trophies · Pinch in for the story ›" and a tap on it opens the timeline too. `openTrans` and `settled` know a group opened from the line (`tl.g`): its tiles are placed at the plaque's spot on the line so the album grows out of it, and Back or a pinch out closes it back onto the line.
- A new level, `view === "timeline"`, with its own touch, mouse, wheel and key handlers on the document in capture (like the trade table): drag or flick sideways, spread to go back (q = 1 − (r − 1) / 1.1, snapped by speed then position), tap a plaque (its album), the wall thumbnail (back), or a milestone (a toast with its line); Escape, Backspace, arrows; ctrl+wheel on a trackpad pinches in and out. Back in the strip and the count both go back to the wall. A lens, a search, a filter, the list or Choose your sets steps out of the timeline first (instantly) and then does its thing, so all of those keep working.
- The line: pixels per day so the whole story is about two screens wide (0.35 to 8 px/day), month ticks, labels per month, quarter, half year or year as room allows, January carrying the year. Plaques hang below the line in date order on a string to their date; a crowded day fans out to the left. Milestones from the data, no new storage: the first card you got, any day 20 or more cards arrived (the childhood binder, an import, Select all), the first chase you made (its moment is in its id), a set finished but kept on the wall (gold), a trade done. Labels end at their tick and step up a lane when one is in the way. The growth curve (how many cards you had, over time) fills the band above the line and rises to meet the wall. The header reads "4 finished, worth $2,391.65. First: Popular in Base Set, Sep 1, 2025." (two lines on a phone), the thumbnail is captioned "Today · Your wall, 638 of 1,327", and a hint along the bottom says how to leave.
- On a phone the thumbnail sits at the right of the screen (today is the right end of the line and a centred thumbnail left no room for a plaque beside it); on a wide screen it sits in the centre. The line can't scroll past today.
- Reduced motion: no shrink; past q = 0.5 the timeline is simply there with the thumbnail, and every settle is instant. Dark mode through `theme` (ink, muted, slot-line, gold, plaque) and the body class `timeline` hides the caption and deal bar.
- `styles.css`: two lines.

## Try this first on the phone
1. Get a trophy or two (Import, then finish a chase with Mark, All, Done, or in a scratch: `__w.markAllInSet()` inside a binder, then age one with `__w.done[key].at -= 86400e3 * 150; __w.layoutAll()`). Back on the wall, put two fingers on the mosaic and close them slowly: the wall shrinks under them toward the right edge while the plaques glide out onto the line. Let go past halfway, or flick. Drag left through the years, tap a plaque to open its album, Back to see it close onto the line, then spread two fingers on the timeline and watch the wall grow back.

## Gesture contract
All checks pass (`npm test -- --variant r16-radical-trophy-timeline`): on a fresh wall nothing is finished, so a pinch on the mosaic does nothing and every other gesture is the base's. With four trophies (scenario script, phone light and dark, desktop, reduced motion): a slow small pinch on the wall stays home, a quick short pinch opens the timeline, a slow small spread on the timeline stays, a quick spread goes back, the line scrolls sideways, tapping a plaque opens its album and Back closes it onto the line, the wall thumbnail and Back go back, a lens tap leaves the timeline, no page errors.

## Frame budget
`npm run test:perf -- --variant r16-radical-trophy-timeline` (fresh wall, phone): mosaic 16.7 ms, held pinch 22.2 ms per frame. With four trophies (scenario, phone, dpr 2): the held timeline pinch 18.9 ms, the open timeline 16.7 ms per frame (one scaled drawImage, the curve, four plaques). On the 1440×900 desktop page the same frames are 44 and 33 ms, under the base mosaic's own 63 ms there (the software canvas at 2880×1800). Making the snapshot costs two mosaic draws once, at the start of the pinch; the plaques draw from their cached engravings as before.

## Unsure about
- The thumbnail at the right of a phone screen rather than the centre the brief asked for: centred, a 110 px wall left 140 px beside it, less than one plaque. The shrink toward the right edge still reads as "into the corner"; worth feeling.
- The wall is drawn once as an image, so while the timeline is up a deal landing doesn't flash in the thumbnail (the lens badge still counts it). Fine for a thumbnail, but it's the one place the wall isn't live.
- A trophy finished on the import gets today's date, so a fresh import's three trophies all hang beside the wall on strings to one point. True to the data; a real import would carry the finish dates.
- The growth curve above the line is an addition past the brief, to give the band above the line something to say (it's the Time filter's curve, in place). If it's noise, it's one function.
- The milestones are read from the data rather than recorded (the first card's `got`, days with 20+ arrivals, the chase id's timestamp), which keeps the variant storage-free but means "cards arrived" counts marks by hand on one day as an arrival too.
- The empty band under the plaques on a phone. The line sits at half height so the curve has room; a taller plaque on the line (its engraving bigger, a line of what's in it) could use the space.
- Back to the wall inside an album opened from the line puts the group back among the panels (the base's behaviour), which closes to the mosaic rather than the timeline. Right, I think: that's what the button says.
