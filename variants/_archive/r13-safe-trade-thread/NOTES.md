# r13-safe-trade-thread

**Round:** 13. After the handshake: what happens to a proposed trade?
**Concept:** A trade is a thread, like a Messages conversation or an eBay offer thread: Shake hands sends the offer and the table stays up with the cards on it, locked, while the trade bar grows into the thread ("You proposed: Hitmonchan for Pikachu and Pikachu. You're up $70.73." then "Waiting on Maya"). Twelve seconds later Maya answers in the same thread: a counter moves the cards on the table and offers Accept and Decline, an acceptance crosses the cards and flies them to their new homes, a decline ends it with her one-line reason, and a completed trade stays as history ("Traded Oct 5"); the chip reads the state and the list view shows the same thread in rows.

## What changed
- Added `76-thread.js` and `styles.css`; nothing replaced or removed, `index.html` untouched (the thread and its buttons are injected into `#tradebar`).
- Records in `wall-trades` gain `state` (proposed, countered, accepted, declined, done), `counter` (the offer as it stands after a counter), `replyAt`, `reason`, `by` (them or you) and `doneAt`. Old records without a state read as proposed. Reset the demo clears them (base).
- The shared reply rule: `scheduleReply` fires 12 s after `rec.at` (rescheduled after a reload, so a proposal from before still gets its answer); `deliver` picks by `h32(`${t.id}|${rec.at}|reply`)`: under 0.45 accept, under 0.8 counter, else decline. It waits while the wall is mid-transition (or, with the table up, while the table itself moves). A counter shifts the balance toward them by one card: Maya drops one of hers from what you get, or adds one more of yours she chases to what you give, whichever the seed picks (the other if that one is impossible; accept if neither is). A decline's reason is seeded too: "I'd want the Magneton too." when there's a spare of yours she chases that you didn't offer, else one of three plain lines.
- Completing (`completeTrade`, on their accept or yours): the cards you gave leave your collection (`setOwned` false, quiet) and stop being spares; the ones you got arrive (`setOwned` true, quiet, dated now) and leave your chase list; one relayout for the batch (`setOwned` is redefined with a `quietLayout` guard). With the table up the cards cross (the base's 520 ms handshake), the bar reads "Traded with Maya / Pikachu is yours", then the table closes with the sides swapped (`closeTable`, `endTable` and a new `swapSides`): what you gave flies up into Maya's chip with her cards, what you got flies down to its new tile on the wall, and the panel's ripple and the given card's un-flood are timed to land when the flight does. With the table closed the wall floods where it sits and the Trade lens flies to its new shape (`liftLayout(true)`).
- The table (`openTable`, `tableLayout`, `cardAt` redefined): an active record's cards are already on the table when it opens; while an offer is out nothing on the table or in the binders moves (`cardAt` returns null); the bar's height is the table's floor, eased, so the binders make room for the thread. A counter arriving with the table up moves the cards with the base's own `place` flights (the dropped card goes home, the asked-for card comes out of your binder).
- The thread (`updateTradeBar` redefined, `renderThread`, `rowsOf`, `rowHTML`): every record for this trader, oldest first, as rows with a time ("just now", "4 min ago"): "You proposed: … You're up $70.73.", "Maya countered: …" (her name in her ink), "Maya accepted." / "You accepted." / "Maya declined: I'd want the Magneton too." / "You declined.", "Traded Oct 5." centred, and a pulsing gold dot with "Waiting on Maya". Rows under 2.5 s old slide in. The rows area scrolls past 136 px. The bottom row keeps the base's meaning (the balance, the counts) with the buttons for the state: Shake hands, Take back (while waiting), Decline and Accept (countered), none while the cards cross.
- `shake` redefined: sends the proposal, the table stays up; the toast's Undo takes it back. `toast` gains a label parameter so the reply toasts off the table say "Maya countered: Hitmonchan for Pikachu." with Open, which flies to the Trade lens if needed and opens the table from her chip.
- The chip (`drawChip`, `stripLayout` redefined): "Waiting on Maya" in gold, "Maya countered" in green with a dot on the avatar and a green ring, "Traded Oct 5" in green, otherwise the base's "Wants 7 of yours". A declined thread goes back to "Wants 7 of yours". Traders with a thread stay in the strip and the list even with no current match; a trader with something to answer sorts first.
- The list (`drawList` redefined): the Trade section shows the same rows under each trader (a hairline down the left), with Take back, Accept and Decline, or "Propose 5 for 26" (which proposes every match both ways, as before, and starts the same 12 s clock).
- Reduced motion: no crossing or flights, the trade completes in place; the thread rows and the dot don't animate. Dark mode through the CSS variables and `theme`.

## Try this first on the phone
1. Import with "Chase every card I'm missing", open a few cards you own and choose Spare (or write `wall-spares`), then tap **Trade** and Maya's chip. Put one of yours and two of hers on the table and tap **Shake hands**: the thread appears under the table with "Waiting on Maya". Wait twelve seconds. She answers in the thread: if she counters, watch a card move on the table, then tap **Accept** and watch the cards cross and fly to their new homes; if she accepts, the cards cross straight away; if she declines, read her reason and change the offer. Then tap her chip again: the trade is history, "Traded Oct 5", and the table is open for another.

## Gesture contract
All checks pass (dpr 1 and 2). Nothing about tap, pinch, drag or the composed levels changes; the tests run on a fresh wall where nothing is proposed, so no reply ever fires during them. While an offer is out the table's cards and binders don't move (a tap or pull does nothing), by design; scrolling the binders still works.

## Frame budget
`npm run test:perf -- --variant r13-safe-trade-thread`: mosaic 16.7 ms per frame, held pinch 16.7 to 17.8 ms per frame (budget 34; the base measures the same). The thread is DOM, rendered only when a record changes (keyed); the table reads the bar's height once a frame (no layout invalidation at rest) and eases the floor toward it. Nothing in the reply path runs per frame.

## Unsure about
- The round's rule for a counter reads "they drop one of your cards they asked for, or add one more of theirs you chase ... so the balance shifts toward them by one card". The two halves point opposite ways; I followed the balance clause and the concept's own example ("Charizard for Blastoise and Dratini" becoming "Charizard for Blastoise"): Maya drops one of hers, or asks for one more of yours. If the other builders read it the other way the counters won't compare like for like.
- After Shake hands the table stays up ("Waiting on Maya") rather than closing as the base does. It makes the reply land in front of you, but a collector who proposes and leaves sees nothing for twelve seconds except the chip; the reply toast's Open brings them back.
- "Accepted" is a state the record passes through in the same call as "done", so a thread never shows a trade accepted but not yet completed. If a real backend separates them, the rows are already there (the row reads "Maya accepted" then "Traded Oct 5").
- A done trade keeps the chip on "Traded Oct 5" until the next proposal, hiding "Wants 7 of yours". It could revert after a day.
- The thread rows only re-render when a record changes, so "just now" doesn't become "1 min ago" until the next change or reopen.
- While the table is up a live-deal arrival starts a wall morph that can't finish until the table closes (base behaviour, `frame()` returns early); the reply ignores it with the table up but waits on it with the table closed. Worth a look when the table is harvested.
- The list's state line ("Waiting on Maya") uses the base's green; the chip uses gold for waiting.
