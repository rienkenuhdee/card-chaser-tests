# r16-bold-trophy-room

**Round:** 16. Trophies: where do they live after their day on the shelf, and what can a trophy do?
**Concept:** After its day on the shelf a trophy leaves the wall altogether: the case at the end of the wall becomes one row, a door ("Trophy room · 3 trophies · $13k ›" with every plaque's engraving as a thin strip), and tapping it slides the wall away for the room, its own level like the trade table (dark wood shelves, the plaques lit, newest at the top, rows you scroll, Back or a pinch returns to the wall where it was). The new idea is that the room reads as what finishing has been worth: every plaque carries its worth over the last year as a thin line beneath its name, date and worth, and the room's header sums the whole case with the same line; a set's master and grand set trophies stack behind the set's plaque and fan out beneath it on a tap.

## What changed
- Added `82-room.js` (after 80-welcome, so its redefinitions win). State: `room = { on, q, anim, wallScroll, fan, ... }`. While the room is up it borrows the wall's scroll (`mScroll`/`mMax` are the room's; the wall's is kept in `room.wallScroll` and restored on close), and the plaques are laid out in mosaic coordinates, so a tap, a spread, the press ring, the open transition and Back all work on them unchanged.
- Redefined by hoisting:
  - `caseLayout`: the case is one 64 px door row (`trophyCase`, `DOOR` pseudo-block); the trophies' tiles live in the strip along its bottom (`packStrip`), so a trophy coming down from the shelf shrinks into the door and a search ring still finds a card. `layoutAll` adds `roomLayout()` when the room is up (header, rows of plaques, a fanned stack's sub-rows, the shelves), closes the room if nothing is left in it, and drops the trader strip under it. `shelfLayout`: the day-is-up timer waits while the room is open, as it does under the table. `syncShelfPad`: no shelf pad in the room.
  - `hit`: the door on the wall; in the room the plaques, each stack's badge (`g.fanBtn`) and the fanned rows (`r.pick`). Case trophies are not hit on the wall (the door is). `enterGroup`: the door opens the room, a badge toggles the fan, a fanned row switches the set to that view (`setScope`) and opens its album; a plaque opens its sealed album as before. `exitToMosaic`: Back from an album opened in the room closes it into the room; an album that stopped being a trophy (Back to the wall in its header) or a slide to a neighbouring set closes onto the wall (`endRoom` first). `setChrome`: Back shows in the room, `#where` says "Trophy room". `backBtn.onclick`, the count button, Escape and Backspace close the room; a search, a lens or the list closes it instantly (like the table).
  - `pinchMove`, `releasePinch`: a pinch in the room closes it under the fingers (speed first, then halfway, as the table does); a spread on the door opens the room; a spread on a plaque opens its album.
  - `drawMosaic` becomes `drawWall` (the base's loop with the door drawn under the strip's tiles, and case trophies not drawn as plaques) or, with the room up, the slide (the wall translates left 30% and dims under a shadow as the room slides in from the right; `room.q`, 520 ms; reduced motion: it appears) then `drawRoom`. `drawPanel`: a case trophy in the room is `drawRoomPlaque`; on the wall it draws only mid-morph, shrinking through the door. `drawCaseLabel` is a no-op (the door is its label). `readTheme` reads the door and room colours.
  - `trophyListHTML`: the line says "In the trophy room." and the year's change.
- Worth over time (`walkOf`, `seriesOf`, `caseSeries`, `drawWorthLine`): each card's price is walked back twelve months, each month dividing by a step seeded by the card id and the month, with a small lean per card and more swing for higher tiers; a plaque's series is the sum over its cards, cached per plaque by card count and worth, and the room's header sums the plaques (computed at layout time, never per frame). The text is plain: "Up $27 this year", "Down $2.03 this year", "Steady this year".
- Frame budget: the plates (plaque, fanned row, header) are rendered once offscreen with their shadow and lighting and kept per holder (`cachedImage`, keyed by size, dpr, stack, fan state, series and theme), so a room frame is a flat fill, the shelves, one `drawImage` per plate and the engraving tiles. Press states are drawn live on top.
- `styles.css`: door and room colours (the room is the same dark wood in both schemes; the plaques keep the light scheme's gold so they read as lit), and `body.inroom` hides the lens bar, the time bar, the caption and the deal bar while the room is up.
- Debug hook additions: `room`, `openRoom`, `closeRoom`, `caseList`, `toggleFan`, `seriesOf`, `shelfMorph`.

## Try this first on the phone
1. Finish a few sets (Mark, All, Done inside each, or set `wall-owned` and `wall-done` in a scratch script and age them past a day). Scroll to the end of the wall: the case is now one dark row, "Trophy room · 3 trophies · $13k ›", with every trophy's colours as a strip. Tap it: the wall slides off and the room slides in. Read the header's line (what finishing has been worth this year), then tap a plaque: it comes forward and its album opens behind it; Back returns to the room, not the wall. Pinch in anywhere in the room and the wall comes back where you left it.
2. With Base Set finished in both the set and master set views, the plaque shows a second plate behind it and "1 behind ▾": tap it and Base Set fans out beneath; tap the fanned row and that view's album opens.

## Gesture contract
All checks pass (`npm test -- --variant r16-bold-trophy-room`). A fresh wall has no trophies, so no door and the layout is the base's. With trophies (scratch scenario, four sizes and schemes including reduced motion): the door opens the room, the room scrolls, a tap on a plaque opens its album and Back returns to the room, a quick pinch closes the room and the wall is at the scroll it was, Back and the count button close it, a search closes it, Back to the wall in a room-opened album frees the trophy and lands on the wall, and a shelf trophy's day ending shrinks it into the door.

## Frame budget
`npm run test:perf -- --variant r16-bold-trophy-room`: mosaic 16.7 ms per frame, held pinch 22 to 24 ms per frame (fresh wall; the door costs one rounded rect, two strings and a `drawImage` per trophy). Inside the room with three trophies (scenario script, phone 390×844 @2x): room 16.7 ms per frame, a held spread on a plaque 22 to 28 ms per frame. On a 1440×900 @2x desktop the room is 30 ms and the held spread 42 ms, against 60 ms and 71 ms for the base wall itself at that size on the software canvas.

## Unsure about
- The room hides the lens bar (as the table does). Have and Need don't mean much in a room of finished things, but Value could recolour the engravings; it isn't reachable without leaving.
- The worth line is a year of made-up drift. Real data would start at the finish date, and a month-old trophy would have a short line; the header's "this year" would then be "since you finished".
- A fanned view switches the set's scope (as the People chase header's segmented control does) before opening; coming back, the set's panel on the wall keeps that view. That may surprise, or be exactly right.
- The door's strip gives each trophy an equal share of the width; with ten trophies the segments get narrow and the row becomes a colour bar. A capped count ("and 6 more") might read better.
- A live deal landing while the room is up still flashes on the wall; the room drops the line to the Chase button since the bar isn't there. The toast still shows.
- A desktop ctrl+wheel out in the room does nothing (in: opens the nearest plaque's album). Escape closes.
